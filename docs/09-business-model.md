# 9. Business model

> **Principle:** "We make money from the product, never from the data."

## Market

- 355 million menstruating women and girls in India
- Start with college students and young professionals
- Also serve women in perimenopause

## Revenue

| Stream | Details |
|---|---|
| Freemium | Free tracking and insights |
| Ishtar Plus (subscription) | Deeper insights, more languages and condition-focused programs (price to be decided) |
| B2B | Clinics and gynaecologists receive patient-shared summaries; campus wellness partnerships |

## Go-to-market

- College wellness clubs
- Women's health creators
- Privacy as the headline message

## Costs

- No AI inference bills (models run on users' devices)
- Static hosting
- Development
- Model tuning
